I'm Nalini, marine bio and Nat Geo nerd. I'm currently doing my undergraduate at Georgia Tech in Environmental Science, minoring in Biomedical Engineering (BME). My current focus is on building international experiences and practical knowledge before planning to do my masters in marine biology or ecology. I'm specifically interested in shallow-water ecosystems and the changing conditions from anthropogenic stresses.

I grew up in the Bay Area, California and attended Design Tech High School. At d.tech, I practiced design thinking and earned my Innovation Diploma doing a year-long project about the lack of common knowledge about female physiology and how it adversely affects female high school athletes. 

I love trail running, rock climbing, and generally being active and outside. Definitely missing California and the Half Moon Bay sunsets!

This summer, I'm looking forward to taking Microbiology, spending time with family, picking up slack lining, and preparing for my semester abroad. I'll also be continuing contributing to the [Great Reef Census](https://greatreefcensus.org/) as a citizen scientist. In Fall 2026, I will be an International Exchange Student at the University of Galway in Galway, Ireland. There, I'm hoping to fulfill most of my BME minor requirements. 

One thing that's really important to me is gaining as much practical experience as I can while in college. I want to spend as much time abroad as possible, taking advantage of GT's exchange and study abroad programs during the academic year, and pursuing internships and research opportunities over the summers. 

I'm hoping that this platform will help me stay connected with everyone while I travel and study abroad. I'm excited to share updates about my adventures, and I fully expect this to go through many iterations as I find my voice and figure out the best way to do this :).
